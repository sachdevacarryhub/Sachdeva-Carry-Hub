# Admin guide — running the showroom website

A simple guide for the store owner and staff. No technical knowledge needed.

## Signing in

1. Go to **yourwebsite.com/admin** (for example `https://sachdevacarryhub.in/admin`).
2. Enter your email and password → **Sign in**.
3. To sign out: **Sign out** at the bottom of the left menu (on phones, tap ☰ first).

Forgot your password? Ask your developer to reset it, or change it any time in **Settings**.

## The menu

| Menu item             | What it's for                                                   |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dashboard**         | Totals and the latest products                                  |
| **Products**          | Add, edit, hide or delete products                              |
| **Categories**        | Trolley Bags, School Bags, Ladies Purses — and any new ones     |
| **QR Codes**          | Stickers for products and a QR for the website                  |
| **Store Information** | Phone numbers, WhatsApp, address, Instagram, map link, logo     |
| **Settings**          | Change your password; check that the website is set up properly |

## Adding a product

1. **Products → Add product**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Product name** (required) — e.g. *Premium Black Trolley Bag 24"*.
   - **Category** (required).
   - **Short description** — one line shown on product cards.
   - **Description** — more detail, can be several paragraphs.
3. **Images** — tap **Choose images** (or drag photos in). You can add up to 12.
   - Photos are automatically made smaller and faster before uploading — just use the camera
     photo as-is.
   - The ⭐ image is the **main photo** shown on cards. Tap ⭐ on another photo to change it.
   - Use the ← → arrows (or drag) to change the order.
   - Wait until every photo finishes uploading before saving.
4. **Features** — short highlights, e.g. *Material → ABS*, *Wheels → 4 Wheel*. Tap
   **Add feature** for each.
5. **Specifications** — detailed table, e.g. *Size → 24 inch*, *Lock → TSA Lock*.
6. **Available colours** — tap the quick colours, or type your own.
7. **Visibility** (right side):
   - **Active** — on = visible on the website.
   - **Featured** — shows in *Featured Collection* on the home page.
   - **New Arrival** — shows in *New Arrivals* on the home page.
8. **Create product**.

Tips: good photos sell. Use plain backgrounds, good light, and show front, back, inside and
wheels/straps.

## Editing, hiding and deleting

- In **Products**, the switches in each row turn **Featured**, **New** and **Active** on/off
  instantly.
- ✏️ edits the product. ↗ opens it on the website.
- To take a product off the website **temporarily** (out of stock), switch **Active** off. Its
  QR sticker will show "not available" until you switch it back on.
- 🗑 **Delete** removes it permanently, including photos. Its QR sticker stops working forever.
  Prefer switching it off.
- ⚠️ Changing the **URL slug** of a product changes its web address — its printed QR sticker
  must be reprinted. The form warns you.

## Categories

- **Categories → Add category**: name, description and a nice cover photo.
- Switching a category **off** hides the category **and all its products** from the website
  (nothing is deleted).
- A category that still has products can't be deleted — move or delete its products first.

## QR codes

**QR Codes** page:

- **Website QR** — for visiting cards, the shop counter, banners, carry bags.
- **Product QR** — search and select a product, then:
  - **PNG** — for WhatsApp or phone,
  - **SVG** — best for a printer/sticker shop (stays sharp at any size),
  - **Print** — prints a ready sticker with the product name.

Stick each product's QR on its tag. Customers scan → see photos and details → tap WhatsApp or Call.
Always test-scan one sticker before printing many.

## Store Information

Everything customers see for contacting you comes from here: store name, owner, phone numbers,
**WhatsApp number** (used by every WhatsApp button), address, city, state, pincode, Instagram link,
Google Maps link, logo and favicon. Save, and the website updates right away.

Google Maps link: open your shop in Google Maps → **Share** → **Copy link** → paste.

## What the website does *not* do

No online payments, orders or delivery — customers enquire on WhatsApp, call, or visit the store.
